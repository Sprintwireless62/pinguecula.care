Ask a question

Describe what you are noticing, such as the spot, when you first saw it, and how your eye feels, and we will point you toward the right next step. If you would like an interpreter, a support person or a longer appointment, please say so.

This is not an emergency service. For sudden vision loss, severe pain, an eye injury or a chemical splash, call 000 or go to an eye emergency department. In Victoria, that is the Royal Victorian Eye and Ear Hospital, open 24 hours.[16]
