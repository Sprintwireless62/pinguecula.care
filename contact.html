Contact & help

Ask a question

Describe what you're noticing — the spot, when you first saw it, and how your eye feels — and we'll point you toward the right next step. If you'd like an interpreter, a support person or a longer appointment, just say so.

This is not an emergency service. For sudden vision loss, severe pain, an eye injury or a chemical splash, call 000 or go to an eye emergency department — in Victoria, the Royal Victorian Eye and Ear Hospital (24 hours).
